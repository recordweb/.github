# RecordWeb Members

This file lists institutions registered as **Members** of the RecordWeb Community Group, per [`GOVERNANCE.md` §3.4](GOVERNANCE.md).

Membership is not a legal relationship — it is a declaration of professional engagement. Members provide use cases, test scenarios, and domain requirements; participate in pilot implementations; and review specification drafts from an institutional perspective. Members do not have voting rights on technical decisions, and do not need to sign the W3C Community Contributor License Agreement unless they contribute normative specification text directly.

This is distinct from being a **Contributor** (an individual, joined via the [official W3C Community Group page](https://www.w3.org/community/recordweb/), bound by the CLA). An institution can have staff who are Contributors, be registered as a Member, or both — the two roles are independent.

**To register:** open an issue using the [Member Registration template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/member-registration.md). A Chair will confirm registration within 10 working days and add the institution below.
